Ayah marker icon
================

Title:  Rub el Hizb
Author: Delapouite
Source: https://game-icons.net/1x1/delapouite/rub-el-hizb.html
File:   https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rub-el-hizb_-_Delapouite_-_game-icons.svg
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The icon is used as the verse-number marker on the mushaf pages. The vector drawable
res/drawable/ayah_marker.xml was produced from the original SVG by normalising the path
to absolute coordinates and removing the two circles that form the central ring, so the
ayah number can sit in that space. The octagram, its corner ticks and its edge notches
are used unchanged.

game-icons.net asks that the attribution be visible in the product; it appears in the
Settings screen under "Credits" and in this file.
